The water cycle in slow motion · Grade 10 · Unit 1 · Lesson 1

1 · Evaporation The Sun heats seas, rivers and lakes, so liquid water changes into water vapor. Plants add vapor by transpiration, and living organisms by respiration and excretion.
2 · Condensation Rising vapor cools and changes back into tiny liquid droplets. The droplets gather and form clouds.
3 · Precipitation Droplets grow heavy and fall as rain or snow. Snow stays frozen on cold mountain tops.
4 · Surface flow & infiltration Water runs over the land to rivers and the sea. Some leaks through soil pores and sedimentary rocks and forms groundwater.
5 · Collection Groundwater moves slowly toward the sea. The cycle never stops: it is a nearly closed system, so Earth’s water is reused again and again.
